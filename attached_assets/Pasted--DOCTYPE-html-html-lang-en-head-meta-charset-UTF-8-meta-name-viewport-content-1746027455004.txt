  <h1>FAQ: Legal AI-Generated Content on DeepTubeAI.com</h1>
  <p>Welcome to DeepTubeAI.com, your platform for sharing AI-generated videos in a safe, legal, and creative community. Below, we answer common questions about what types of AI-generated content, including deepfakes, are permitted on our platform. Our goal is to foster ethical and entertaining content while strictly adhering to laws and our <a href="/terms">Terms of Use</a>.   <div class="faq-section">
    <h2>1. What is AI-generated content?</h2>
    <p>AI-generated content refers to videos created or enhanced using artificial intelligence tools, such as text-to-video generators (e.g., Runway, Pika), AI avatars (e.g., Synthesia), or deepfake technology. On DeepTubeAI.com, this includes videos in our 12 entertainment categories: Sci-Fi, Comedy, Animation, Music, Horror, Romance, Action, Surreal, Parody, Interactive, Historical, and Kids.</p>
    <p>Examples include animated shorts, virtual music videos, or interactive stories created with AI. All content must comply with our strict guidelines, and <strong>nudity or explicit material is strictly forbidden</strong>.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-section">
    <h2>2. What types of AI-generated content are legal to upload?</h2>
    <p>You may upload AI-generated videos that are original, consensual, and compliant with all applicable laws, including U.S. laws (e.g., DMCA, TAKE IT DOWN Act) and international regulations (e.g., EU AI Act, UK Online Safety Act). Legal content includes:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>AI Avatars</strong>: Videos featuring synthetic characters or avatars for storytelling, comedy, or music videos, such as a virtual singer in a Music video or a cartoon character in a Kids’ animation.</li>
      <li><strong>Text-to-Video Animations</strong>: Videos generated from text prompts, like surreal art for the Surreal category or action scenes for the Action category, provided they don’t depict real individuals without consent.</li>
      <li><strong>Voice-Over Videos</strong>: AI-generated voiceovers narrating stories, such as a Kids’ fairy tale or a Parody skit, using tools like Lyrebird AI.</li>
      <li><strong>Full-Body Synthetic Videos</strong>: Videos with entirely AI-generated characters, like sci-fi aliens in the Sci-Fi category or historical figures in the Historical category, with proper consent if resembling real people.</li>
      <li><strong>Interactive Videos</strong>: Choose-your-own-adventure stories or gamified content in the Interactive category, created with tools like Elai.io.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>All content must be free of nudity, explicit material, or illegal elements (e.g., violence, hate speech). Creators must verify that their videos are original or use licensed/public domain assets (e.g., music, images).</p>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-section">
    <h2>3. Are deepfakes allowed on DeepTubeAI.com?</h2>
    <p>Deepfakes—videos that use AI to manipulate or swap faces, voices, or likenesses—are only permitted if they are <strong>ethical, consensual, and clearly labeled as synthetic</strong>. Legal deepfakes include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Consensual Parodies</strong>: Spoofs or humorous videos in the Parody category, such as an AI-generated celebrity likeness with their explicit permission (e.g., a comedic movie scene reenactment).</li>
      <li><strong>Historical Reenactments</strong>: Videos in the Historical category recreating figures (e.g., Abraham Lincoln), provided they are fictionalized, labeled as AI-generated, and don’t misrepresent living individuals.</li>
      <li><strong>Fictional Characters</strong>: Deepfakes creating entirely fictional personas for Sci-Fi or Comedy, with no real-person likenesses involved.</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Prohibited Deepfakes</strong>: Non-consensual deepfakes, including those depicting real individuals without their explicit written consent, are strictly forbidden. This includes revenge porn, deepfakes intended to deceive (e.g., political misinformation), or any content that harms reputations. Nudity or sexually explicit deepfakes are also banned.</p>
    <p>Creators must submit metadata or affidavits confirming consent for any deepfake depicting a real person’s likeness, per our Content Guidelines. Violators face immediate content removal and account termination.</p>
  </div>

    <h2>5. What laws govern AI-generated content on DeepTubeAI.com?</h2>
    <p>We comply with strict legal standards to ensure all content is lawful, including:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>U.S. DMCA</strong>: Protects against copyright infringement. Upload only original or licensed content, and report violations to <a href="mailto:dmca@deeptube.co">dmca@deeptube.co</a>.</li>
      <li><strong>TAKE IT DOWN Act (2024)</strong>: Mandates removal of non-consensual deepfakes or intimate content within 48 hours. We promptly remove such material upon report.</li>
      <li><strong>Section 230</strong>: Grants us safe harbor from user content liability, provided we act swiftly to remove illegal material.</li>
      <li><strong>EU AI Act</strong>: Requires labeling of AI-generated content to prevent deception. All deepfakes must be marked as synthetic.</li>
      <li><strong>UK Online Safety Act</strong>: Obliges us to prevent illegal content, including revenge porn or CSAM, and implement age verification.</li>
      <li><strong>State Revenge Porn Laws</strong>: Most U.S. states criminalize non-consensual intimate content. We ban such material and support victim reports.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Creators must ensure their content complies with these laws and local regulations in their jurisdiction. We use AI detection tools and human moderators to enforce compliance.</p>

      <li><strong>Label AI Content</strong>: Clearly mark AI-generated videos as synthetic in the title, description, or video itself (e.g., “AI-Generated Parody”).</li>
      <li><strong>Use Licensed Assets</strong>: Ensure music, images, or other elements are original, licensed, or public domain. Unlicensed content violates DMCA.</li>
      <li><strong>Avoid Nudity</strong>: Do not include nudity, explicit content, or material that could be deemed offensive or illegal.</li>
      <li><strong>Verify Fictional Content</strong>: Ensure AI-generated characters or scenarios don’t mimic real individuals without permission.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Check our <a href="/content-guidelines">Content Guidelines</a> for full details. Use tools like Runway or Synthesia ethically, and consult a legal professional if unsure about your content.</p>
